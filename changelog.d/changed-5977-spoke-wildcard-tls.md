- Hosted spokes can now be served by the fleet wildcard certificate instead of minting one certificate per hive ([#5977](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/5977)). A wildcard covering `*.<cluster domain>` already existed and served nothing: every provisioned spoke Ingress carried its own `tls:` block, and an explicit `tls:` block always wins over the ingress controller's `--default-ssl-certificate`, so the wildcard was inert while each hive kept issuing its own certificate — measured at ~195 Ingress TLS references and ~60 `Certificate` objects across two clusters on 2026-09-04, against a Let's Encrypt cap of 50 issuances per week per registered domain. With ~263 wildcard-covered hostnames on those clusters that made every hostname change cost a certificate, which is what exhausted the quota during the 2026-09-03 incident. Setting `wildcard_tls_secret` on a cluster in `clusters.json` now makes provisioned spoke Ingresses omit both the per-host `tls:` block and the `cert-manager.io/cluster-issuer` annotation — the first is what beats the default certificate, the second is what makes cert-manager mint the per-host Certificate at all. It is opt-in per cluster and off by default, because omitting the `tls:` block on a cluster whose controller has no `--default-ssl-certificate` makes ingress-nginx serve its built-in **self-signed** certificate and fails TLS on every spoke dashboard at once; the two cluster prerequisites are documented in [Serving spokes from the fleet wildcard certificate](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/blob/v4/src/docs/spoke-wildcard-tls.md). Coverage is decided per host rather than per cluster and follows the RFC 6125 single-label rule, so a host the wildcard cannot cover — the domain apex, a deeper name, or one a level above the wildcard's scope such as `dibs.hivecommons.dev` ([#5925](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/5925)) — keeps its own certificate even on an opted-in cluster. OpenShift Route clusters are unaffected: their Routes use edge termination and never minted a per-host certificate through this path.
