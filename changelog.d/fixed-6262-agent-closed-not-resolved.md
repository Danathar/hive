- **The Advisory Digest no longer reports bare closes as resolved.** Advisory agents (notably `guide`) re-check their own findings and `bd close` the ones they judge fixed. The digest rendered that judgement as a struck-through "resolved" entry even when nothing had changed, so still-open documentation gaps appeared under **Recently Resolved** ([#6262](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/6262)). **Recently Resolved** now lists only closes the hive has evidence for: a merged PR, closed GitHub references, or a healed access check. Every other close appears under a separate **Recently Closed — Fix Not Verified** section, without strikethrough. The collapsed-overflow line and the zero-findings summary account for unverified closes hidden by the changelog cap, so neither claims "resolved" for them.
