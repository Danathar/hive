- The advisory digest's top-N no longer spends its scarce slots on findings it then tells the reader not to trust ([#2364](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/2364)). The renderer captions a finding as not-current for three reasons — its file is gone at the analyzed commit ([#3704](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/3704)), its evidence was computed at some other commit ([#5130](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/5130)), or it was republished from byte-identical cached text ([#5236](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/5236)) — but only the first one influenced which findings won a slot, and provenance staleness was not even computed until after the cap had already chosen. So a finding nothing had re-checked could displace one that WAS confirmed at the commit the digest is stamped with. The live 2026-09-04 digest showed the cost: of the ten findings it rendered out of 302, three carried an outdated or unverified caption, including two of the four CRITICAL slots. Ranking now demotes on all three signals alike. It stays a demotion, not a filter: unverified means nobody re-checked the finding, not that it is fixed, so an unverified finding still backfills any slot no confirmed finding of equal severity claims, still keeps its caption when it does, and an unverified critical still outranks a confirmed low — severity continues to dominate.
