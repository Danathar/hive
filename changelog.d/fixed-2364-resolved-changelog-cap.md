- The advisory digest's **Recently Resolved** changelog now honours `governor.advisory.max_findings` instead of rendering up to 100 healed findings regardless ([#2364](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/2364)). The cap bounded only the open findings, so the section a reader opens the digest *for* became the small part of it: the live 2026-09-03 digest rendered 10 open findings in 4,937 characters — under a note saying 286 more existed — and 100 already-fixed ones in 22,138, making 82% of the comment work that was already done. Withheld resolved entries are now announced the way withheld findings are (*"…plus 20 more resolved in the last 48h"*), `show_all: true` lifts the cap off the changelog as well, and the 100-entry ceiling that keeps the comment inside GitHub's character limit still applies above it — stated now rather than trimming in silence.
