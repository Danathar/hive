- Upgrade targeting is now channel-aware: a spoke whose Deployment tracks a release channel (`:stable`, `:candidate`, `:edge`) is told to reach the commit that channel currently carries, not the newest merged SHA on the release branch ([#5994](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/5994)). Since the stable soak policy landed, per-merge publishes move `:candidate` only and `:stable` advances hourly after a 24h soak, so branch HEAD is a digest a `:stable` spoke's own tag is deliberately withholding — the spoke rolled, re-pulled `:stable`, reported the SHA it started on, and the hub re-sent the identical instruction until the retry budget was gone. 41 spokes were in `UPGRADE FAILED` this way, with 90 of 97 tracking `:stable`. The hub now resolves the channel tag to the commit it was built from (the `org.opencontainers.image.revision` label, the same evidence `promote-stable.sh` reads), targets that, and treats a channel spoke sitting at that commit as up to date. When a channel cannot be resolved at all the hub instructs nothing and says so, rather than falling back to a target it knows the spoke cannot reach.
