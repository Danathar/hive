- The dashboard's GitHub API card no longer reports full headroom while the App installation's budget is spent ([#5733](https://github.com/kubestellar/hive/issues/5733)). A just-minted installation access token transiently reports a fresh, empty bucket for a budget that is genuinely shared across every token for the installation, and hive latched that reading — so `.ghRateLimits.core.remaining` moved *backwards* within a window and sat pinned at the limit for minutes at a time. Measured on a live spoke: the card claimed 100% headroom while ~89% of a 7100/hr budget was consumed, peaking at 814 requests of divergence, and the reporter cut a spoke's cadence on the strength of it. Rate-limit readings are now clamped to be monotone within a window: a value may only fall until the window's reset has actually passed in wall-clock time, so a re-minted token describing its own empty bucket can no longer be mistaken for a budget rollover. The card also shows when the reading was taken ("Measured 2m ago", amber past five minutes) and `/api/status` carries a new `ghRateLimits.core.observed_at`, because `reset` moves independently of the sample and could not distinguish a held value from a fresh one.
